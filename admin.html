System Guide

Staff operating guide for the complete IPGS Admission V2 journey — from application and Academic Consultant handoff until Active Student.

LIVE V2 · UPDATED 18 SEP 2026
Core rule: follow the workflow status shown by the system. Do not manually skip a gate. Screening is a recommendation, SAC is the admission decision, Acceptance unlocks SKY activation, SKY activation unlocks Orientation, and final student access is sent only after Academic Handover and all provisioning tasks are completed.

Detailed End-to-End Flow

Who acts · what to do · what happens next
01
Application Submitted
→
Student Completes the online Admission Form and uploads required documents. System: creates the student Drive folder, Admission Form PDF, workflow record, acknowledgement email and PG-ADM-01.
02
Academic Consultant / Prospect Setup
→
Consultant If the application came through a consultant link / selected consultant, the consultant receives the prospect email. Create the student as a Prospect in SKY, enter the SKY Prospect ID, select the approved Fee Group, then Submit. Registry For a direct applicant, use Prospect / Activation → Set Prospect and complete the same fields.
Admission review continues in parallel while Prospect / Fee Group is being prepared
Document Review→Qualification Screening→Ready for SAC
03
Document Review
→
Registry Open Applications → Open → Documents. Verify uploaded documents and complete the document review. PG-ADM-01 must use the controlled one-page layout. If required, use Regenerate PG-ADM-01. For PhD, Preliminary Research Intent may remain Outstanding and is followed up separately; it does not block SAC assignment or the next admission stage.
04
Qualification Screening
→
Registry Review AI/document screening where available, then confirm Field Relationship and Relevant Work Experience. Complete the formal qualification screening. Important: the screening recommendation does not replace the SAC decision.
05
SAC Review
→
Registry / SAC Create or select the SAC session, assign eligible candidates and prepare the SAC pack. PG-ADM-01 is the first controlled document in the pack. After the physical/manual SAC meeting, Registry records the authorised decision: Direct Entry, Internal Assessment, or Rejected. PREREQ is not selected directly at SAC.
06
IA / PREREQ (if required)
→
Registry / Academic Direct Entry bypasses this stage. IA candidates complete the Internal Assessment process. Where the approved IA outcome requires a prerequisite course, PREREQ is handled after IA. Only a completed eligible route may proceed to Offer.
07
Official Offer Letter
→
Registry When the case is Eligible for Offer, generate and send the Official Offer Letter. The system records the PDF URL, issue status and timestamp. Do not issue before the admission route is complete.
08
Electronic Acceptance
→
Student Opens the Acceptance link, reviews the required documents and signs electronically. System: saves signed acceptance documents and timestamp, then updates Acceptance Status = ACCEPTED.
GATE A — SKY Activation: Prospect Status must be completed, SKY Prospect ID and Fee Group must exist, and Acceptance Status must be ACCEPTED. Before that, activation stays locked.
09
Registry SKY Activation
→
Registry Open Prospect / Activation. Cases move from Prospect Pending → Waiting Acceptance → Ready to Activate. Complete the activation in SKY, then click Activate in SKY and enter the SKY Student ID / Registration No. The system records activation status, date and officer.
GATE B — Orientation: student must be both ACCEPTED and SKY ACTIVATED. An accepted but non-activated student must not be assigned to Orientation.
10
Orientation
→
Registry Create the Orientation session, select eligible students and assign them. The student receives the invitation immediately. Automatic milestone reminders are scheduled at 3 days, 2 days, 1 day and approximately 1 hour before the session. Students assigned later receive only the reminders still relevant to their timing. Mark attendance after the session; ATTENDED becomes the readiness gate for Academic Handover.
11
Academic Handover
→
Registry Create an Academic Handover batch for attended students and send it to Academic. Academic Opens the handover link and accepts the batch. Acceptance creates the provisioning work.
12
IT / Moodle / e-Library Provisioning
→
PICs IT completes Innovative Email, Moodle PIC completes Moodle access, and e-Library PIC completes e-Library access. Registry monitors the Provisioning queue until all three are complete.
GATE C — Active Student: completion of the three provisioning tasks alone does not make the student Active. Registry must send the final Student Access email.
13
Send Student Access → ACTIVE STUDENT
✓
Registry Click Send Student Access, enter the temporary credentials and send the access email. Temporary passwords are not stored in the Admission spreadsheet/audit. After successful send, the workflow becomes ACTIVE_STUDENT and the Academic Portal record is activated.

How staff use each module

Screen → action → expected result

Dashboard

  1. Check Action Center and pending KPIs.
  2. Identify bottlenecks by stage, programme and intake.
  3. Use Refresh if the figures are stale.

Applications

  1. Search by name, reference, ID/Passport or email.
  2. Click Open for the applicant drawer.
  3. Use Overview, Documents, Screening, SAC/Assessment, Offer & Acceptance and Activity.

Academic Consultants

  1. Add/maintain consultant records from the consultant page.
  2. Share the unique referral link.
  3. Referral-link consultant is auto-selected/locked.
  4. New consultants append as a new record; they must not overwrite the previous consultant.

PG-ADM-01

  1. Generated automatically after application.
  2. Controlled layout is the approved one-page table form.
  3. Applicant biodata is auto-filled.
  4. Yes/No, Evidence/Remarks, route and signatures are completed by authorised staff/SAC.
  5. Use Regenerate only when a refreshed copy is required.

SAC Monitoring

  1. Create session and assign eligible candidates.
  2. Print/prepare SAC pack.
  3. Record the authorised physical/manual SAC result.
  4. Finalize the session after all decisions are entered.

IA / PREREQ

  1. Track only candidates routed to IA.
  2. Record assessment/interview/result progress.
  3. Apply PREREQ only after IA when required by the approved route.
  4. Complete the route before Offer.

Offer & Acceptance

  1. Issue only when Eligible for Offer.
  2. Monitor Offer status and Acceptance status.
  3. Use signed PDFs saved in the student folder as the record.
  4. Acceptance must be ACCEPTED before SKY activation.

Prospect / Activation

  1. Prospect Pending: consultant/Registry must complete Prospect ID + Fee Group.
  2. Waiting Acceptance: no activation yet.
  3. Ready to Activate: complete SKY activation and enter Student ID.
  4. Activated: student becomes Orientation-eligible.

Orientation

  1. Create session.
  2. Select Accepted + SKY Activated students only.
  3. Assign student; invitation is sent immediately.
  4. Automatic reminders: 3 days → 2 days → 1 day → approximately 1 hour before.
  5. Late assignment receives only the next relevant reminder(s).
  6. Mark Attended after the session.

Academic Handover

  1. Create batch after Orientation attendance.
  2. Send to Academic.
  3. Monitor acceptance.
  4. Academic acceptance triggers provisioning tasks.

Provisioning

  1. Monitor Innovative Email, Moodle and e-Library.
  2. When all three complete, status becomes Ready to Notify.
  3. Registry sends Student Access.
  4. Only then does the student become Active.

Reports

  1. Use funnel and programme/intake/referral distributions.
  2. Export filtered Applications to CSV when needed.
  3. Use Reference No as the main identifier in internal follow-up.

Decision & workflow gates

Never bypass these controls

Troubleshooting & good practice

Check these first
  • If numbers look outdated, click Refresh.
  • If an applicant cannot be found, clear filters and search using Reference No or ID/Passport.
  • Open student Drive folders/PDFs from the applicant drawer instead of browsing manually.
  • Use the application Reference No as the primary identifier for internal discussion.
  • If PG-ADM-01 looks different from the approved one-page table layout, do not use it for SAC; regenerate and report the issue.
  • For PhD, an outstanding Preliminary Research Intent is a follow-up remark only and must not be used to block SAC or subsequent admission processing.
  • Do not treat screening recommendation as the final SAC decision.
  • Do not select PREREQ directly at SAC; it follows IA where applicable.
  • Do not activate in SKY before Acceptance = ACCEPTED.
  • Do not assign Orientation before SKY Activation = ACTIVATED.
  • Do not mark a student Active merely because accounts are created; send Student Access first.
