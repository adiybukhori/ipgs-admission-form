# Campus RPG V8 — High-detail Chibi Full Prototype

V8 retains the V7 premium campus layout and V6 hybrid individual/cohort workflow, but replaces the small pixel characters with custom vector chibi actors rendered directly in Phaser.

## V8 upgrades
- Larger high-detail chibi staff and student characters.
- Expressive faces, hair styles, eye highlights, blush, uniforms, backpacks and staff lanyards.
- Smooth anti-aliased rendering instead of tiny pixel-character rendering.
- Limb-based walking animation with arms, legs and subtle head/body bob.
- Dedicated sitting pose for Orientation and staff workstations.
- Direction facing while walking.
- Dynamic y-depth while characters move, improving the 2.5D feeling.
- Applicant name labels are subdued until hover.
- Larger speech-bubble spacing for the new character scale.
- Slower, smoother walking retained.
- Purpose-built V7 rooms and the cohort Orientation workflow remain intact.
- Production Admission V2 remains unchanged.

The V8 chibi actors are programmatic vector art, so the prototype does not depend on a new third-party character sprite license.
