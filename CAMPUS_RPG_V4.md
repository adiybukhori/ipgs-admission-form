# Campus RPG V4 — prototype foundation

This branch is a separate playable prototype and does not replace the production Admission V2 flow.

## Open-source components
- Phaser 3 — MIT License — https://github.com/phaserjs/phaser
- Kenney Roguelike Characters / Indoors / City assets — CC0 1.0
- Public asset mirror used by the prototype: https://github.com/Two-Weeks-Team/openClawWorld/tree/main/packages/client/public/assets/kenney

## V4 behavior
- Large top-down RPG world with camera pan / zoom
- Five applicant characters queued at Admission
- Multiple visible specialist NPCs inside every process room
- Staff escort applicants through a protected central corridor
- Sub-task agents activate visibly inside each room
- Direct Entry, IA and Prerequisite routes
- Read-only connection to existing /api/admin-data
